Where to post wholesale real estate deals

Give buyers enough information to evaluate a property—and a clear way to continue the conversation.

Start with a complete property post

Include the location, asking price, property type, available photos, known condition and the timing you can substantiate. State what you know and what remains unverified. Label estimates as estimates; a polished post cannot replace due diligence.

Choose a channel built for your audience

Generic social groups can introduce people, but conversations and property information can become scattered. Better Real Estate brings property posts, a real-estate network, messaging and deal workflows together. Use multiple appropriate channels while keeping one clear source for your property details.

Post and share through Better

Create a free account, choose your real estate roles and open Post to start a blank property post. Add your authorized photos and details, publish, then use the property’s Share action to distribute its branded link. A recipient can open the property on Better and continue through the platform’s available tools.

Keep the opportunity current

Update photos and details when conditions change, respond to questions and keep your availability accurate. Share only properties and information you are authorized to market. Publication does not guarantee buyers, offers or a closing.
