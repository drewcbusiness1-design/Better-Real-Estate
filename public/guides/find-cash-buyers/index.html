How to build a cash-buyer network for off-market properties

Look for fit, then verify capability. A list of names is not the same as a qualified buyer network.

Define the property before finding the buyer

Be clear about location, price range, property type, condition and timeline. Different investors buy for different strategies. A buyer who likes one market or renovation level may have no interest in another.

Ask what buyers actually purchase

Discuss target markets, budget, property types, repair tolerance and timing. Keep criteria current. An expression of interest is not proof of funds, an accepted offer or a commitment to close.

Use Better to organize the conversation

Choose Buyer / Investor and Seller / Wholesaler roles as appropriate. Use Network to connect with real estate participants, publish a property and review available buyer criteria and matching tools. Message through the platform and track follow-up with the deal workflows available to your account.

Verify before relying on a commitment

Independently verify identity, funding and the transaction details with appropriate professionals. Do not publish someone’s financial documents or private contact details. Better is a platform for connections and information, not a guarantee that any participant is qualified.
