# Better Real Estate discovery setup

This release prepares the website; it does not guarantee rankings or submit external accounts. No outreach, ad spending, reviews or production deployment performed.

## After Netlify successfully deploys
1. Open https://betterrealestate.org/guides/ and all three linked pages on mobile. Confirm the exact logo and free-account link.
2. Open a real active property, use Share, and paste that link into a private message draft. Check its branded preview; published availability and authorized photos only. Do not manufacture a sample listing. Existing previously cached previews may need a platform refresh.
3. In verified Google Search Console, submit sitemap.xml and inspect https://betterrealestate.org/guides/ plus the three article URLs. Request indexing if available. A request is not guaranteed indexing or ranking. Check Pages and Performance over time rather than repeatedly requesting indexing.
4. In Bing Webmaster Tools, add the site or import your verified Search Console site, follow its ownership-verification flow and submit the same sitemap. No new DNS or account changes are included in this ZIP.
5. Check robots.txt publicly; its wildcard allowance permits OAI-SearchBot as well as ordinary search crawlers. Hosting/firewall restrictions can still block them. This release adds no AI manipulation text, fake reviews, authority or ranking claims.
6. Add the real site and guide links to the LinkedIn company page and existing Instagram/Facebook/TikTok profiles. Keep names, official logo and description consistent. No new social account is created here.

## A practical weekly distribution loop
- Publish one real demonstration: create an authorized property post, inspect evidence or connect with someone who has agreed to participate. Hide private information, tokens, documents and financial/contact details. State membership requirements for paid tools.
- Use the mascot creatives to earn attention, then link to the corresponding useful guide or real workflow. Never imply fictional takeover images depict actual adoption.
- Invite existing approved affiliates to use their OWN tracked Affiliate Center link. Give them the materials below; do not replace the tracked link with an untracked guide URL when attributing signups.
- Respond usefully to relevant real questions where community rules permit. Do not bulk spam, scrape contacts or invent recommendations/testimonials.
- Review actual signup/activity charts and Search Console clicks. They are different measurements; this release does not add conversion attribution or claim causality.

## Affiliate / creator starter material
Only approved participants who have accepted current program terms have active tracked links. Rates, bonuses and eligibility follow the existing program, not this guide. Paid endorsements must be disclosed as required by the platform and applicable rules; this toolkit does not hide compensation.

Short introduction: I’m sharing Better Real Estate, a platform for people working off-market properties. You can join free, post a property and connect with real estate participants. Here’s my affiliate link: [insert your own approved tracked link]. I may earn a commission from eligible purchases through it.

Demo outline: Show a blank Post form → add real authorized property details → publish → show the Share action → explain how a recipient opens the property on Better. Do not show fake buyers, matches, earnings or completed transactions.

Founder introduction: I’m Andrew, founder of Better Real Estate. I’m building a real-estate-only network for property posts, buyer connections and deal workflows. It’s free to join. If you try it, tell me what helps and what needs work.

Follow-up: Here’s the site: https://betterrealestate.org/. Start by adding your real estate roles and posting a property you’re authorized to market. No card is required to join; some tools depend on membership.
