# Better Real Estate v29.12 — Deal Intelligence Reliability

- Fixes the ~30-second Deal Builder failure caused by chaining live property/web research and AI synthesis inside one serverless request.
- Splits Deal Intelligence into two bounded stages: research first, AI synthesis second. Each stage gets its own serverless execution window.
- Runs independent Regrid, authorized MLS/RESO, and public-web research concurrently rather than serially.
- Adds bounded source timeouts: Regrid 8s, each MLS request 10s, web research 20s. A slow optional source degrades with a visible source error instead of killing the whole analysis.
- UI now shows Step 1 of 2 (research/cross-reference) and Step 2 of 2 (AI synthesis), so the user can see what Better is doing.
- Keeps the top-line evidence rules from v29.11: verified facts first, sold comps for ARV, active/pending only as market context, provenance/conflicts visible, no fabricated property facts.
