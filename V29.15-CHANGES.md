# Better Real Estate v29.15 — Production Deal Intelligence

This release replaces the repeated Deal Builder patch cycle with one evidence-first production workflow designed for correctness, provenance, bounded runtime, and low avoidable Netlify/API usage.

## Property identity
- A submitted full address now uses Regrid's ranked `/parcels/address` endpoint first. That endpoint already returns full parcel records and Regrid relevance scores, so Better avoids an unnecessary Typeahead + UUID round trip on normal lookups.
- Address matching normalizes street suffixes, cardinal directions (`West`/`W`, `North`/`N`, etc.), unit text, locality, state and ZIP before a parcel can become the subject. Common full-address pastes without commas are normalized too, so city/state text cannot poison street matching.
- Better requires an exact house/street guard plus either a strong local match or strong Regrid relevance score. Wrong-parcel matches remain release blockers.
- Regrid Typeahead is retained only as an optional Enterprise rescue path behind `REGRID_USE_TYPEAHEAD=true`. It is disabled by default so accounts without that product do not spend a failed request on every property.
- The workflow uses a new cache namespace so stale v29.14 evidence is not reused.

## Property truth
- Beds, baths, living area, year built, and property type retain field-level provenance.
- One high-quality source is retained as **Recorded** evidence, but the primary fact remains **Needs confirmation** until independently corroborated. The raw source value stays inspectable and cannot unlock a precise valuation by itself.
- Independent agreement promotes a field to verified only when at least one corroborating source is stronger than a syndicated portal. Multiple portal pages alone cannot turn a repeated upstream error into verified truth. Source conflicts remain visible and disputed values are withheld.
- A single-source property record alone cannot unlock a precise valuation.

## Public web corroboration
- Public-web research uses the existing OpenAI Responses API web-search tool only when deterministic evidence still needs corroboration or sold comps.
- The research model defaults to the cost-sensitive `gpt-5.6-luna` independently of the main synthesis model, uses no extra reasoning effort for structured evidence extraction, and can be overridden with `OPENAI_RESEARCH_MODEL` without requiring another key.
- When both subject-fact corroboration and sold-comp fallback are needed, Better performs one combined web-research call rather than two separate paid searches.
- Web-search requests explicitly request the consulted-source list. Better accepts a claim only when its URL was actually surfaced by web search and the property/address requirements pass.
- Zillow is never directly scraped or queried through an unauthorized API. A Zillow URL may be used only when legitimately surfaced through hosted public web search or later through separately approved/licensed access.

## Comps and ARV
- Configured authorized MLS closed sales remain first priority.
- Regrid nearby recorded sales are queried only after a subject parcel is identified.
- Public-web sold-comp discovery runs only when deterministic closed-sale evidence is insufficient.
- The comp engine rejects stale/distant/duplicate/low-similarity/outlier records, scores recency/distance/size/type/beds/baths/year, and applies a capped partial living-area adjustment instead of naive 1:1 $/sq-ft extrapolation. A precise ARV additionally requires independently established comp distance: normally at least 2 distance-verified nearby candidates among 3 usable closed sales, or 2 unusually strong recent/nearby distance-verified comps. Unknown-distance web comps may remain visible evidence but cannot unlock precise ARV.
- If those distance-verification and subject-identity gates are not met, ARV stays withheld rather than presenting unsupported precision.

## AI role
- AI is the final synthesis layer, never the source of record.
- It cannot invent comps, sale facts, source citations, or choose disputed property facts.
- Final synthesis is skipped when the property/comp gates fail.
- Final synthesis is cached by evidence fingerprint so reopening an unchanged property does not spend another AI call. If synthesis itself times out/fails, Better still returns the verified deterministic evidence/comp result, does not consume the user’s successful-analysis allowance, and stores a 30-minute synthesis-failure cooldown so repeated clicks do not immediately burn more AI calls.

## Netlify/API cost controls
- Property research is cached for 24 hours.
- Forced refresh is protected for 30 minutes.
- Concurrent identical research coalesces in-flight.
- The browser never resends its own evidence into final valuation; the server uses cached evidence.
- Normal Regrid identity is one ranked-address request rather than Typeahead + UUID.
- Public-web research is at most one search call for a property research pass when both facts and comps are missing.
- Reopening the same successfully analyzed property does not consume another Free/Plus new-property allowance and does not create duplicate successful-analysis analytics rows.

## Integrity and diagnostics
- Admin Research Diagnostics exposes cached identity, Regrid match candidates/scores, field evidence, MLS, nearby-sale, web, and comp-gate stages without triggering another provider call.
- Regression coverage includes the production failure address `503 W Grand Prairie St, Palestine, IL 62451`, cardinal-direction normalization, Regrid relevance scoring, conflict withholding, source-provenance rejection, comp gating, server-owned evidence, caching, and quota behavior.

## 2026-09-29 — Deep background Deal Intelligence reliability upgrade

- Deal Intelligence research no longer depends on a short synchronous browser/API request. A Netlify Background Function owns fresh research and can run for the longer serverless background window while the UI shows staged progress.
- The existing `@netlify/blobs` dependency stores resumable job state; no new provider or paid dependency was introduced.
- Regrid address lookup now follows Regrid's documented address-search pattern more closely: street address + state `path` first, with one bounded full-address fallback only when the safe-match gate does not pass.
- If Regrid/MLS do not establish subject identity, exact-address public-web evidence can establish identity without weakening field truth: one strong county/public-record source may support identity, otherwise Better requires at least two distinct exact-address web sources. Individual beds/baths/sqft/type values still obey Recorded/Verified/conflict rules.
- Public-web research is split into a subject-fact pass and, only when still necessary, a sold-comp pass. Both have materially longer quality-first time budgets and stronger search context. Deterministic evidence still runs first.
- Regrid nearby-sale research no longer buys up to 100 arbitrary nearby parcels. It uses the parcel query endpoint with subject geometry/radius plus `saleprice > 0` and a recent `saledate` floor, capped at 40 records, so paid parcel returns are concentrated on plausible closed-sale evidence.
- When public web research discovers sold comps without trustworthy distance, Better ranks the strongest candidates and uses bounded Regrid address lookups on only the top six to independently establish coordinates/distance. Unknown-distance web comps can remain visible but cannot unlock precise ARV.
- Fresh research is persisted server-side before synthesis. Reopening the same property reuses the 24-hour evidence cache; the 30-minute forced-refresh guard remains.
- The browser polls only lightweight job state with backoff instead of repeatedly rerunning Regrid/OpenAI research.
- Tutorial / What's New version is 45.
- Property-truth rules remain unchanged: one source may be shown as Recorded, conflicts stay visible, and precise ARV/AI synthesis remain gated by verified identity and defensible closed-sale evidence.

## v29.15 COMPLETE DEAL INTELLIGENCE — final acceptance repair (tutorial v48)
- Tutorial / What's New is now **48**. This release is the authoritative continuation of the v29.15 multi-source architecture.
- The live 1905 Drexel Dr regression is now a permanent fixture: Better must not show sourced core facts while leaving the investor snapshot blank, and it must not clip the source-count metadata inside narrow fact cards.
- Core fact presentation now distinguishes **facts surfaced** from **independently established** facts. Recorded non-conflicting values stay visible to the investor without being silently promoted to Verified; conflicts remain withheld from trusted subject facts.
- Evidence cards use a three-column desktop layout with source/status metadata stacked safely inside each card. Source labels may wrap naturally but must never be clipped, truncated by the card, or pushed outside the container. Tablet/mobile intentionally step down to two/one columns.
- Public property research remains multi-source first. The initial major-property-site pass researches both subject facts and sold comps. If facts are still weak, the next pass is unfiltered and explicitly seeks independent property, assessor, tax/public-record and brokerage evidence while preferring sources outside already-seen domains/source families.
- A low-confidence working ARV no longer ends research prematurely. When a working range exists but the precise gate has not passed, Better still performs the bounded deeper sold-comp pass to look for explicit comparable distances and stronger evidence. If precision is still unavailable and comp evidence remains concentrated in one source family, the remaining bounded pass targets independent comp corroboration.
- Common public sold-date formats such as `10/21/25` and `Oct 21, 2025` are accepted when the evidence text visibly supports the same sale date. This fixes valid comps being silently discarded because a page used a two-digit year.
- The subject property's own prior transfer is explicitly excluded from its ARV comp pool. Distressed/as-is sales and statistical price outliers remain excluded from normal retail ARV.
- Recorded but non-conflicting beds/baths/living area/year may be used only as **comparison context** for deterministic comp similarity and repair planning. They do not become Verified subject facts and are never allowed to resolve a conflict.
- Precise ARV keeps the normal three-comp / two-distance gate. The inherited two-comp exception is now calibrated for truly strong evidence: two recent closed sales within 0.75 miles with similarity >=75 may unlock precise ARV, with a visible warning that additional comps would strengthen confidence. This matches the permanent rule allowing two unusually strong recent/nearby distance-verified comps.
- When distance support is missing, three recent reasonably similar closed sales (similarity >=60) may produce only a clearly labeled **Working ARV range**. It remains Low confidence when source diversity is weak and never masquerades as precise.
- Deterministic ARV display is separated from the stricter AI narrative gate. When exact-address identity and a defensible precise/working comp result exist, Better displays ARV, repair planning, 70% MAO, projected flip spread and projected project cost even if final AI prose remains withheld pending stronger independent subject-fact corroboration. This avoids blank investor math while preserving the Property Truth Gate.
- The research cache remains server-owned and cost-protected under namespace `v29.15-complete-intelligence-r5`: 24-hour evidence reuse, 30-minute forced-refresh protection, duplicate-job reuse, maximum four bounded web passes, no paid research on ordinary page reload, and no quota consumption for failed/withheld analyses.
- No new provider, scraper, paid dependency, or client-trusted evidence path was added. Regrid remains optional corroboration; configured authorized MLS/RESO remains preferred when available; public web research remains the default multi-source discovery layer.
