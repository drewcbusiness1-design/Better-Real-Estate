# Better Real Estate v29.13 — Property Truth Gate

## Why this release exists
A live property lookup can contain stale or incorrect source data. Better must prefer **no verified value** over a confidently wrong value. A reported 4 bed / 4 bath property that is actually 3 / 2 is the regression case for this release.

## Deal Intelligence changes
- Exact-address matching is scored before Regrid/MLS subject records can participate in analysis.
- Core subject facts — bedrooms, bathrooms, living area, year built, and property type — require independent corroboration before they are promoted as verified facts. A single Regrid, MLS, broker, or portal value remains visible as evidence but is not silently treated as truth.
- Conflicting sources are preserved in the Property evidence panel and the disputed field is withheld unless a stronger multi-source consensus exists.
- AI is forbidden from resolving a disputed raw value on its own. Server-side verified fields overwrite any model-supplied core fact.
- A precise working ARV now requires BOTH sufficient verified subject identity and at least two credible researched sold comps that survive the deterministic comp engine. Otherwise ARV is withheld.
- When the truth/comp gate is not satisfied, Better skips the final AI synthesis call entirely instead of spending OpenAI/Netlify resources on an analysis that would be withheld.
- Insufficient/withheld analyses do not consume a Free/Plus successful-analysis allowance.

## Cost controls
- Research results are cached by normalized property address for 24 hours.
- Manual refresh is protected for 10 minutes to prevent accidental repeat API/Netlify spend.
- In-flight duplicate research requests share the same source work.
- Cached research shows its timestamp in Deal Builder.

## Acceptance rule
Blank / Needs confirmation / Conflicting is preferable to displaying a wrong property fact. This rule is permanent.
