- Affiliate commissions are now explicitly 30% ONE-TIME on the referred customer's first eligible paid membership transaction only. Renewals, later invoices, cancellation/resubscription and plan changes do not create another commission.
- Affiliate hold period changed from 14 days to 5 days.
- Affiliate terms advanced to `2026-09-27-v2`; approved affiliates must affirmatively accept the updated terms before earning under them.

- Marketplace seller proceeds remain real cash and the Wallet now explicitly separates seller proceeds earned, withdrawable cash, and Better Credits.
- Stripe Connect remains the approved payout architecture. Better Real Estate never stores raw bank/debit-card credentials.
